# Additional preview and search dependencies

The Word content preview and Chinese-initial search include these unmodified libraries:

- **OpenMcdf 3.3.0** — Copyright 2010–2026 Federico Blaseotto and Jeremy Powell. Licensed under MPL-2.0. Corresponding source: https://github.com/openmcdf/openmcdf/tree/11b5d876cdebb472f1845dfa55e9e9b953aed65f. License: `licenses/OpenMcdf-MPL-2.0.txt` in application output (`docs/licenses/` in source).
- **TinyPinyin 1.1.0** — hstarorg; based on promeG/TinyPinyin. Licensed under MIT. Source: https://github.com/hstarorg/TinyPinyin.Net. Full copyright and license: `licenses/TinyPinyin-MIT.txt` in application output (`docs/licenses/` in source).

These components read local document containers and provide a local pronunciation dictionary. They do not upload files, execute Office macros, or require an Office installation.
